Frequently asked

Post-operative recovery care, answered directly.

What we do, what we do not do, and how booking works. If your question is not here, text (480) 296-1331.

Do you provide medical care?

No. Serenity Haus provides non-medical post-operative support: transportation, positioning, garment and dressing assistance, hydration, nutrition, mobility support, medication schedule reminders and observation as directed by your surgeon. We do not diagnose, treat or replace your surgeon or physician.

Which procedures do you support?

Most commonly cosmetic and elective surgery recovery, including body contouring, breast surgery, facial procedures and combined surgeries. If you are unsure whether your procedure fits, ask before booking.

What is included in Surgery Day Recovery?

Transportation from the surgical facility, help settling in safely, post-operative positioning, garment and dressing support, vital-sign observation, medication schedule assistance, hydration, nutrition, mobility assistance, and communicating concerns per your surgeon's instructions. It is $595.

What is included in Private Overnight Recovery?

Continuous in-home support through the evening and overnight: comfort and positioning, scheduled observations, medication reminders, drain assistance, ambulation, hydration, nutrition, garment support, and preparation for the following morning. It is $750.

What is Extended Recovery Care?

Flexible daytime, evening or additional overnight visits beyond the first 24 hours, customized around your procedure, mobility, support system and your surgeon's post-operative plan. It is $550 per visit.

Can I combine services?

Yes. Most patients combine surgery day with the first overnight, then add extended care if they have less help at home.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as your surgery date is confirmed. Care is one to one, so the same person is not scheduled for two patients at once, and popular dates fill.

Where do you provide care?

Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and West Palm Beach, including the surrounding neighborhoods in each. If your address is just outside those areas, ask anyway.

Do you follow my surgeon's instructions?

Yes. We ask for the post-operative instructions in advance and follow them, including drain guidance, garment schedules, medication timing and mobility limits.

What happens in an emergency?

We call 911. Serenity Haus does not provide emergency or clinical care, and any concern that looks urgent is escalated to emergency services immediately.

Do you work with surgeons and surgical coordinators?

Yes. We receive written post-operative instructions, follow them, and report concerns back the way the practice asks.

Do you require a deposit?

Plans are confirmed once availability is verified for your date. Payment details and any deposit are explained at booking.

Plan your recovery before surgery day.

Tell us your procedure date and city. We will confirm availability and build a recovery plan around your surgeon's instructions.
