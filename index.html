The hours after surgery are the ones nobody plans for.

Common questions

Before you book.

Do you provide medical care?

No. Serenity Haus provides non-medical post-operative support: transport, positioning, garment and dressing help, hydration, nutrition, mobility assistance, medication schedule reminders and observation as directed by your surgeon. We do not diagnose, treat or replace your surgeon or physician.

Which procedures do you support?

Most commonly cosmetic and elective surgery recovery, including body contouring, breast surgery, facial procedures and combined surgeries. If you are unsure whether your procedure fits, ask us before booking.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as your surgery date is confirmed. Dates are limited because care is one to one, and the same person is not scheduled for two patients at once.

Can you work with my surgeon's instructions?

Yes. We ask for your post-operative instructions in advance and follow them, including drain care guidance, garment schedules, medication timing and mobility limits.
