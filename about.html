About

Meet Serena.

Experienced surgical care with a deeply personal approach to recovery.

My career in healthcare has been shaped by years of hands-on patient care, with experience spanning medical assisting, surgical coordination, operating room support, recovery and post-operative care.

Working alongside surgical patients throughout their journey has given me an understanding of something that is often overlooked: what happens after surgery matters just as much as preparing for it. I have seen firsthand how overwhelming the first hours and days after a procedure can be, especially once a patient leaves the surgical setting.

Medications, drains, mobility, garments, surgeon instructions, hydration, comfort, and simply having someone knowledgeable nearby can make an enormous difference in the recovery experience. My approach is attentive, discreet and highly personalized. Exceptional post-operative support is found in the details: anticipating what you may need, helping you stay organized and comfortable, and being steady when the day is not.
